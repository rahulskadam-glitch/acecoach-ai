# Sport Intelligence Pack Standard

A sport pack is production-ready only when it includes:

1. **Taxonomy** — sport, discipline, action and phase identifiers.
2. **Capture contract** — camera angle, frame rate, distance, lighting and quality checks.
3. **Metric contract** — units, landmark dependencies, confidence and acceptable ranges.
4. **Scoring contract** — age/level-aware scoring with explainable component weights.
5. **Knowledge pack** — reviewed coaching principles, common faults, corrections and drills.
6. **Model policy** — supported CV and language models, fallbacks and data-region constraints.
7. **Evaluation pack** — labelled clips, expected outputs, safety checks and regression thresholds.
8. **Report templates** — athlete, coach and academy views.
9. **Versioning** — semantic version and reproducible model/prompt/config trace.
10. **Governance** — owner, review date, evidence sources and change approval.
