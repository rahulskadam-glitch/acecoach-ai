# Competitive User-Review Incorporation — v3.2.0

This release adds a governed review-incorporation dimension alongside the competitive capability scorecard.

## Method

Recurring themes were synthesized from public app-store reviews, official product materials, specialist reviews, and forums across SwingVision, Sportsbox 3D Golf, Onform, CoachNow, Skillest, Mustard, HomeCourt, FullTrack, Dartfish, and related sports-analysis products.

Reviews are not treated as ground truth. They can be duplicated, outdated, incentivized, or unrepresentative. A theme is counted as incorporated only when AceCoach includes a concrete feature, mitigation, test, disclosure, or governed feedback loop.

## Review themes

1. Reliability and trust
2. Easy capture and setup
3. Clear, understandable insights
4. Actionable drills and next steps
5. Visual learning and comparison
6. Processing speed and transparency
7. Progress that proves improvement
8. Coach collaboration
9. Mobile and device convenience
10. Value, pricing, and support

## New v3.2 responses

- Review-driven benchmark page with evidence notes and open gaps.
- Robust SciPy signal smoothing and peak detection for movement segmentation.
- Repetition rhythm profile, robust outlier detection, phase-timing distribution, and movement-chain radar.
- Longitudinal Progress Intelligence page combining execution, repeatability, capture quality, and practice completion.
- Product-level feedback form classified by journey stage and issue category.
- Server-write-only feedback storage and a restricted aggregate learning view.
- Installable PWA manifest and mobile metadata.

## Important boundary

The review-incorporation score measures coverage of recurring user needs. It is not an app-store satisfaction score, retention metric, or proof that each mitigation is best in class.
