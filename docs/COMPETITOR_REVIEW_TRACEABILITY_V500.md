# Competitor user-review traceability — v5.0.0

The detailed source research and prior instructions are preserved in `SOURCE_REQUIREMENTS_AND_USER_REVIEW_INPUT_V500.txt`. Neutral, specific Reddit/forum and app-store feedback is weighted above brand testimonials or sponsored content.

## SwingVision

**Valued:** dead-time removal, highlights, easy setup, match statistics, sharing, watch controls.  
**Criticized:** wrong shot/bounce detection, unreliable speed/line calls, stuck processing, lost media, failed export, battery/storage burden, ecosystem restriction, support delay, price-versus-reliability.

**AceCoach response:** original-video preservation and download; upload retry record; SHA-256 duplicate protection; movement confirmation; no-score reliability gates; visible processing stages; safe diagnostics. Racket/ball tracking and thermal validation remain open.

## Sportsbox AI

**Valued:** detailed movement data, 3D visualization, goal ranges, before/after measurement.  
**Criticized:** steep learning curve, excessive data, unclear interpretation, needing a coach, original video not prominent.

**AceCoach response:** player-first four-section report; clean video; one primary correction; technical depth collapsed; methodology labels; plain-language coaching. Validated 3D is not claimed.

## Onform

**Valued:** comparison, slow motion, frame stepping, voice-over, annotations, athlete organization, parent/coach collaboration.  
**Criticized:** confusing navigation, hidden recording, poor scrubbing, drawing/scrub conflicts, limited automatic diagnosis.

**AceCoach response:** visible Upload, Library, Practice, Progress, Methodology, Support routes; frame step and phase step; clean comparison. Voice-over exists for summary; full coach annotation workflow remains open.

## CoachNow

**Valued:** remote communication, private spaces, media-rich coaching history.  
**Criticized:** crashes, mobile playback issues, cumbersome messaging, delayed feedback.

**AceCoach response:** session-linked report, plan, questions/sharing foundation; support diagnostics and planned review states. Offline comments and full coach SLA workflow remain open.

## Dartfish

**Valued:** professional frame analysis, timing, angles, overlays, tagging, reports, multi-sport depth.  
**Criticized:** complexity, manual work, price, overkill for players.

**AceCoach response:** professional depth stays in collapsed Advanced Analysis; normal athlete journey remains automated and four-section.

## Skillest

**Valued:** remote coaches, asynchronous lessons, personalized drills, history, follow-up.  
**Criticized:** variable coach quality, slow turnaround, videos buried in chat, import limitations.

**AceCoach response:** structured session record; dedicated library; practice plan linked to report; coach-quality marketplace and SLA metrics remain open.

## Mustard

**Valued:** simple scorecard, visual cues, parent-athlete language, weakness-linked drills, quick feedback, visible progress.  
**Criticized:** narrow coverage and unclear plan differences.

**AceCoach response:** one correction, one cue, measurable success target, two drills maximum, reassessment, centralized entitlement preview, explicit scientific boundaries.

## HomeCourt

**Valued:** polished design, real-time feedback, automatic tracking, challenges, history.  
**Criticized:** battery/heat, basic or narrow drills, multiplayer uncertainty, slow updates.

**AceCoach response:** practice destination and progress evidence; real-time counting and thermal testing remain open and must not launch without validation.

## Baseline Vision

**Valued:** setup, accuracy, immediate feedback, transparent removal of unreliable metrics.  
**Criticized:** cost, missed events, connection interruption, limited independent evidence.

**AceCoach response:** Validated/Beta/Proxy/Unavailable framework, no-score gate, service health and methodology path. Independent validation remains a release gate.

## FullTrack, Stupa, PlaySight

**Valued:** ball/object tracking, tactical outputs, multi-angle video, facility workflows.  
**Concerns:** device restriction, operational cost/complexity, questionable metrics, limited neutral evidence.

**AceCoach response:** multi-sport object adapters are an architectural requirement; pose-only analysis is not presented as complete sport intelligence.

## Balltime

**Valued:** fast upload/processing, player identification, automatic highlights, export.  
**Criticized:** wrong player, subscription confusion, watermarks, aggressive cropping, missing clip endings, language and playback issues.

**AceCoach response:** original footage preserved; movement/athlete correction remains a key backlog; no watermark on original media; transparent entitlements; playback regression gate.

## V1 Sports

**Valued:** slow motion, model comparison, remote coaching.  
**Criticized:** recording friction, manual import, paywalls, lost data, auto-renewal and refund disputes.

**AceCoach response:** direct upload; media download; payment disabled until renewal, cancellation, export, refund, and historical-access policy are production-ready.

## Cross-competitor release priority

1. **Trust breakers:** wrong AI, lost media, crashes, hidden charges, unsupported certainty.
2. **Adoption drivers:** easy capture, honest processing, simple explanation, visual proof, manual correction, cross-platform access.
3. **Retention drivers:** one priority, guided practice, reassessment, progress narrative, coach oversight, parent access, responsive support.
