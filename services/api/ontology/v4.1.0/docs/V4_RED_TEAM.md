# v4 Red-Team: failure modes that would make AceCoach look intelligent but coach badly

1. **Pose theater** — many joints/angles are shown because the detector has them. Fix: player-mode visual-load limits and thesis-driven overlays.
2. **False contact precision** — frame index presented as exact impact at 30 fps. Fix: contact interval + confidence.
3. **Pro-copy fallacy** — one elite player's geometry becomes a universal target. Fix: self-best/context-matched ranges first.
4. **Symptom coaching** — finish/racket path is corrected although spacing/timing caused it. Fix: EMD + causal role + symptom suppression.
5. **Context collapse** — defensive and attacking reps are averaged. Fix: context signature and do-not-merge rules.
6. **Advice churn** — different primary correction every upload. Fix: cross-session cue stability and promotion rule.
7. **One-rep psychology** — a single good/bad swing becomes a habit claim. Fix: sample gates.
8. **Precision laundering** — uncertain monocular 3D appears as crisp exact overlays. Fix: uncertainty-semantic rendering.
9. **Metric without meaning** — angles are reported without tennis consequence. Fix: suppress metrics with no causal/performance interpretation.
10. **LLM hallucinated biomechanics** — prose creates measurements/causes. Fix: claim traceability + structured evidence IDs.
11. **No falsification** — every hypothesis only seeks confirming reps. Fix: required counterexamples/falsification conditions.
12. **Feedback overload** — 7 faults and 6 drills. Fix: one primary insight, one active cue, progressive disclosure.
13. **Good-style penalty** — valid technique family difference scored as defect. Fix: technique-family/context gates.
14. **Static-pose serve analysis** — trophy position dominates. Fix: event-sequence and timing relationships.
15. **Visual misalignment** — ghost body overlay is seductive but geometrically invalid. Fix: alignment gate; fallback to side-by-side.
