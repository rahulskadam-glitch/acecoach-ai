# v4.1 Player Language — Before / After

This document shows the difference between an evidence-correct analytics sentence and the player-facing coaching sentence AceCoach should render. The evidence is preserved underneath; only the expression changes.

## Two-handed backhand spacing

**Analyst surface**

Lateral contact spacing falls below the player's successful band on deep-ball reps, followed by a steeper late racket path.

**Player coach surface**

Your turn is on time. The space disappears after it. That last adjustment gets you too close, so the racquet has to rescue contact. **Feel: Make room, then swing.**

**Video captions**

The turn is on time. → The last step closes space. → Your hands get jammed. → Make room before you swing.

## Serve toss

**Analyst surface**

The toss cloud shows repeatable lateral drift and lower contact-height proxy on the drifting cluster.

**Player coach surface**

Your motion has good rhythm. The toss is making you chase sideways, so you lose some of the reach you have on your better serves. **Feel: Place it, then reach up.**

**Video captions**

The rhythm is good. → The toss drifts here. → Now you're chasing it. → Contact loses height. → Place it, then reach up.

## Return of serve

**Analyst surface**

Backswing amplitude exceeds the player's successful return cluster at higher incoming serve speeds.

**Player coach surface**

Your split is fine. The backswing is stealing the time you need for contact. Let the serve bring you more of the pace. **Feel: Small turn, early contact.**

**Video captions**

The split is on time. → The backswing keeps going. → That steals contact time. → Turn smaller and meet it early.

## Rule

The player never loses access to the technical evidence. They simply do not have to decode the technical evidence before they can understand the lesson.
