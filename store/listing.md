### Description

Athlentra Tennis helps players understand and improve their technique from a short video.

Record or upload a forehand, backhand, serve, volley, slice, overhead, or footwork clip. Athlentra reviews the visible movement, identifies the strongest supported coaching priority, and turns it into a focused practice plan.

Key features:

- Tennis-specific video and movement analysis
- Key-moment playback and clear visual explanations
- Confidence and camera-quality guidance
- One prioritized correction instead of an overwhelming scorecard
- Practice drills connected to the observed movement
- Comparable reassessments that track progress fairly
- Private video library with deletion controls
- Coaching conversation grounded in the stored report

Athlentra provides educational sports coaching and does not diagnose injuries or replace a qualified medical professional.

### Full description

Improve your tennis technique with feedback grounded in your own video.

Athlentra Tennis analyzes short clips of your forehand, backhand, serve, volley, slice, overhead, and footwork. It explains what is visible, shows the key moments behind its feedback, and gives you one focused priority to practice next.

RECORD OR UPLOAD
Capture a short tennis clip in the app or choose one from your library. Simple filming guidance helps you produce a more reliable analysis.

UNDERSTAND YOUR STROKE
Review movement-specific observations, key moments, confidence, capture limitations, and the measurements supporting each conclusion.

PRACTICE WITH PURPOSE
Follow drills connected to the observed movement and use clear cues during your next session.

TRACK REAL PROGRESS
Record again from a comparable angle and Athlentra will show trends only when the sessions are suitable for a fair comparison.

CONTROL YOUR DATA
Your videos remain private to your account. Download or delete your original media and manage optional data-use choices from your account.

Athlentra is an educational tennis coaching tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis or injury treatment.
